# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Engineers and researchers verifying RAG-generated answers (EEA chatbot context and internal testing). They arrive with an answer and its source documents already in hand, and want to know which claims hold up. Sessions are short, repeated, and task-focused: paste, verify, inspect, repeat.

## Product Purpose

Verify every factual claim in an AI-generated answer against its source documents. The pipeline extracts atomic claims, verifies each against the evidence, and scores the answer 0 to 10. Success means the user can trust the verdict at a glance and audit any individual claim's evidence chain.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, rigorous. A research instrument, not a marketing page. The interface shows its work: real backend stages, real claims, real offsets. Nothing decorative earns space.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS onboarding forms: centered hero, big headings, decorative cards, duplicated CTAs.
- Flashy AI-demo UIs: gradient text, neon glow, animated sparkles, fake progress bars.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence over decoration: every visible element reports something real from the pipeline.
2. The verdict chain is the concept: CLAIM, EVIDENCE, SOURCE, VERDICT relationships drive the layout.
3. Honest progress: only render stages, claims, and scores the backend actually reported. Never fabricate.
4. Calm density: a tool users repeat daily; reduce vertical waste, favor tight hierarchy over whitespace theater.
5. Accessible without color: verdict states carry icons, labels, and text, not hue alone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA: keyboard-operable highlights and panels, visible focus, aria-live on verification progress.
- Verdict states distinguishable without color (icons plus labels).
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
